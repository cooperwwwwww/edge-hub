Precision & aim helpers

Your stick reports 256 steps. Hi-res finds the positions between those steps by averaging the stick's tiny flicker, for smoother, finer slow aim. Fast moves still pass through instantly.
When you let go of the stick quickly, its spring can whip past center and nudge your aim the wrong way. This cancels that overshoot.

Polling rate

Over Bluetooth, Edge Hub asks the controller to report as fast as you pick here.

Over USB, the controller itself sends 250 reports a second, and no app can change that. To reach 1000 Hz on USB you need a USB overclock driver such as hidusbf. Set the controller's device to 1000 Hz in it, then check the measured number above. Not every third-party controller handles it; if yours acts up, set it back to 250.

Game output (virtual Xbox controller)

While remapping is on, games can see two controllers: your real one and the virtual one. If you get double inputs, turn off Steam Input for the game, or use HidHide to hide the real controller from games.

App & reliability

Edge Hub opens quietly in the tray when you sign in, so your settings are always on, as if they were saved on the controller.
Closing the window keeps Edge Hub running (tray icon by the clock). Right-click the tray icon to quit. 

        

        

          Hide your real controller from games recommended
          
With remapping on, games can see two controllers, your real one and Edge Hub's, and sometimes listen to the wrong one. That's why it can seem to only work after replugging. HidHide (free, by the makers of the virtual-controller driver) hides your real controller from games, so they only ever see Edge Hub's. Edge Hub itself can still read it.
